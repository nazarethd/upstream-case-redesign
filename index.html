Upstream

Upstream is a carbon management platform for manufacturing enterprises with international operations.

The SaaS covers three core flows: GHG calculation, procurement of renewable assets/offsets, and disclosure reporting. The company partnered with Ramotion to create a strong brand identity and design a product experience that would simplify complex ESG processes while standing up a functional MVP for market launch.

About

Carbon Solutions Group has been trading environmental commodities since 2006, 250M+ renewable energy credits, 10M+ tons of carbon offsets, and a top-three position in the U.S. market.

Its Fortune 500 customers kept surfacing the same problem: they were paying consultants to calculate their emissions liability, then going elsewhere to buy the credits that offset it.

Upstream is the answer to that request. It assesses a company's greenhouse gas liability across its operations, opens a marketplace of projects CSG sources and develops, and produces the disclosure reporting auditors require - in one system.

We built the brand and the product from the ground up, starting from a name and nothing else. The design had to serve a specific buyer: manufacturers, shippers, and industrial operators with real physical footprints, a segment the existing carbon platforms had largely skipped in favor of big tech.

Challenge

The carbon management market is full of technically complex, enterprise-focused tools like Watershed and Persefoni.

These platforms often require lengthy onboarding, deliver slow pricing quotes, and can be inaccessible for companies without dedicated sustainability teams.

Upstream’s challenge was to:

  • Deliver instant quotes for multiple global carbon markets.
  • Provide a clear, approachable interface for manufacturing clients who may not have deep ESG expertise.
  • Launch a fully functional MVP by early Q4 2023 with both branding and platform design ready for market.
  • Build API integrations to enable in-platform renewable certification management, particularly through the I-REC international registry via the Evident API.

Solution

Our work covered both branding and product development, ensuring a consistent experience from first impression to in-platform interaction.

Brand Identity

We modernized the recognizable carbon bubbles into a scalable visual system, supported by a professional color palette and typography designed to balance technical credibility with accessibility. This identity worked across digital and offline applications, from dashboards to marketing assets.

UI/UX Design

We carried out a full-cycle UX research and design process, ensuring the product was not only functional but also easy to adopt in real-world enterprise settings:

  • Stakeholder interviews to clarify business requirements and expectations.
  • Proto-personas to capture user types and their core needs.
  • Competitive analysis to identify pain points in existing ESG platforms.
  • User journeys and process-experience mapping, paired with an object-oriented UX (OOUX) approach, to align system architecture with real-world user tasks.
  • Interactive prototypes, enabling early validation of flows for emissions calculation, marketplace procurement, and reporting.
  • Usability testing, including System Usability Scale (SUS) evaluation, to benchmark effectiveness and identify friction.
  • QA testing of design implementations to ensure component consistency and edge-case coverage.

This rigorous process produced a design library in Figma that mapped 1:1 to React components, ensuring speed and consistency during development.

Product Development & Integrations

Our team handled both frontend and backend development to keep the MVP on track and aligned across all layers of the product. A highlight was the Evident API integration for I-REC Registry management, allowing users to register, edit, and remove renewable energy devices without leaving the platform. We resolved API quirks, handled error states, and stabilized performance through robust QA and tight technical collaboration.

Outcome

The collaboration delivered measurable results:

  • MVP launched on schedule in Q4 2023.
  • A streamlined user journey that shortened the time from emissions calculation to offset procurement.
  • Instant quotes available in multiple carbon markets—cutting down multi-week wait times common in the industry.
  • Built-in I-REC management, removing manual certification workflows.
  • A scalable design system, ensuring the platform could grow without visual or UX inconsistency.
  • A brand identity that balanced modern sustainability messaging with enterprise professionalism, resonating with both operational teams and Fortune 500 decision-makers.
